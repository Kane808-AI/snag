Privacy Policy

Snag, a Brand75 product · Last updated June 2, 2026
Plain-language summary: We store your account ID, the links you submit, and the text we generate from them, so your vault works. We send video content to AI and retrieval vendors to process it. We delete source video files after processing. We don't sell your data. You can ask us to delete everything.

1. What we collect

  • Account info: your Telegram user ID and username (or email if you use a web/app version), and your plan status.
  • Content you submit: the video links or files you send, and the derived output we generate (transcripts, action blocks, summaries) that we save to your vault.
  • Usage data: counts and timestamps needed to enforce plan limits and operate the service.
  • Billing data: handled by Stripe. We receive a customer/subscription reference and plan status. We never see or store your full card details.

2. How we use it

  • To run the service: retrieve the video, transcribe it, read on-screen text, and generate your output.
  • To maintain your searchable saved-ideas vault.
  • To enforce free and paid plan limits and process payments.
  • To diagnose problems and improve reliability.

3. Source video handling

To process a submission we retrieve the video and pass it to AI analysis. We do not re-host or republish source videos. Temporary video files are deleted after processing. We retain only the derived text and the metadata needed to operate your account.

4. Who we share it with

We share data with service providers strictly to operate the service:

  • AI model providers (for transcription and analysis of submitted content).
  • Video-retrieval vendors (to fetch the submitted video).
  • Telegram (message delivery) and Stripe (payments).

We do not sell your personal data or use your content to train our own models.

5. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account data and vault until you delete them or close your account. You can request deletion of your account and all stored output at any time by contacting us. Some records may be retained where required for legal or accounting reasons.

6. Your choices

  • Request a copy or deletion of your data.
  • Stop using the service and cancel any paid plan at any time.
  • Delete individual saved items from your vault.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). Do not use it if you are under that age.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests: support@brand75.com.
